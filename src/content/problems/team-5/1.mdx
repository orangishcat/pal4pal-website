What is the remainder when \\(2^\{202\}+202\\) is divided by \\(2^\{101\}+2^\{51\}+1\\)?

<details class="answer">

<summary>Solution</summary>

<div class="answer-body">

<p><strong>Answer:</strong> \\(201\\)</p>

<div class="solution-text">

<p>Complete the square, then apply the difference of squares:</p>
<p>\\[\begin\{aligned\}2^\{202\}+202&=(2^\{101\}+1)^2-2^\{102\}+201\\&=(2^\{101\}-2^\{51\}+1)(2^\{101\}+2^\{51\}+1)+201.\end\{aligned\}\\]</p>
<p>The product is divisible by the given divisor, leaving remainder \\(201\\).</p>
