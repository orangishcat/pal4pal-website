Isosceles trapezoid \\(ABCD\\) has parallel sides \\(AD\\) and \\(BC\\), with \\(BC\<AD\\) and \\(AB=CD\\). There is a point \\(P\\) in the plane such that \\(PA=1\\), \\(PB=2\\), \\(PC=3\\), and \\(PD=4\\). What is \\(\frac\{BC\}\{AD\}\\)?

<details class="answer">

<summary>Solution</summary>

<div class="answer-body">

<p><strong>Answer:</strong> \\(\frac13\\)</p>

<div class="solution-text">

<p>Reflect \\(P\\) over the perpendicular bisector of \\(BC\\) to obtain \\(P'\\). Then \\(P'A=PD=4\\), \\(P'D=PA=1\\), \\(P'C=PB=2\\), and \\(P'B=PC=3\\). The isosceles trapezoids \\(DAPP'\\) and \\(CBPP'\\) are cyclic.</p>
<p>Apply Ptolemy's theorem to each:</p>
<p>\\[PP'\cdot AD+1\cdot1=4\cdot4,\qquad PP'\cdot BC+2\cdot2=3\cdot3.\\]</p>
<p>Thus \\(PP'\cdot AD=15\\) and \\(PP'\cdot BC=5\\). Dividing gives \\(\frac\{BC\}\{AD\}=\frac5\{15\}=\frac13\\).</p>

</div>

</div>
