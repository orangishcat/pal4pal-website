In triangle \\(ABC\\), \\(BC=6\\) and \\(6\sin A+5\cos B\cos C=9\\). Find the area of \\(ABC\\).

<details class="answer">

<summary>Solution</summary>

<div class="answer-body">

<p><strong>Answer:</strong> \\(6\\)</p>

<div class="solution-text">

<p>Using the product-to-sum identity and \\(B+C=\pi-A\\),</p>
<p>\\[\cos B\cos C=\frac\{\cos(B+C)+\cos(B-C)\}2=\frac\{-\cos A+\cos(B-C)\}2.\\]</p>
<p>The given equation becomes \\(6\sin A-\frac52\cos A+\frac52\cos(B-C)=9\\). The first two terms are at most \\(\sqrt\{6^2+(5/2)^2\}=\frac\{13\}2\\), and the last term is at most \\(\frac52\\). Equality is necessary in both bounds.</p>
<p>Thus \\(B=C\\), \\(\sin A=\frac\{12\}\{13\}\\), and \\(\cos A=-\frac5\{13\}\\). It follows that \\(AB=AC=\sqrt\{13\}\\), so the area is \\(\frac12\cdot13\cdot\frac\{12\}\{13\}=6\\).</p>

</div>
