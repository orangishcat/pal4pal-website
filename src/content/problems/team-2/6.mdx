Compute \\(\cos^4\frac\{\pi\}\{9\}+\cos^4\frac\{2\pi\}\{9\}+\cos^4\frac\{3\pi\}\{9\}+\cos^4\frac\{4\pi\}\{9\}\\).

<details class="answer">

<summary>Solution</summary>

<div class="answer-body">

<p><strong>Answer:</strong> \\(\frac\{19\}\{16\}\\)</p>

<div class="solution-text">

<p>Note that \\(\frac\pi9,\frac\{7\pi\}9,\frac\{13\pi\}9\\) satisfy \\(\cos3\theta=\frac12\\). Let \\(r=\cos\frac\pi9\\), \\(s=\cos\frac\{7\pi\}9\\), and \\(t=\cos\frac\{13\pi\}9\\). Then \\(r,s,t\\) are the three roots of the cubic derived from \\(\cos3\theta=\frac12\\), using \\(\cos3\theta=4\cos^3\theta-3\cos\theta\\).</p>
<p>The PDF gives this hint and the answer \\(\frac\{19\}\{16\}\\).</p>
