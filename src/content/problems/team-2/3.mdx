Let \\(S\\) be the sum of the squares of the divisors of \\(480\\) (including itself). Compute the number of divisors of \\(S\\).

<details class="answer">

<summary>Solution</summary>

<div class="answer-body">

<p><strong>Answer:</strong> \\(108\\)</p>

<div class="solution-text">

<p>Since \\(480=2^5\cdot3\cdot5\\), expanding the following product gives the square of every divisor exactly once:</p>
<p>\\[S=(1+2^2+2^4+2^6+2^8+2^\{10\})(1+3^2)(1+5^2).\\]</p>
<p>Using the geometric series formula,</p>
<p>\\[S=\frac\{4^6-1\}\{3\}\cdot10\cdot26=260\cdot65\cdot21=2^2\cdot3\cdot5^2\cdot7\cdot13^2.\\]</p>
<p>The number of divisors is \\((2+1)(1+1)(2+1)(1+1)(2+1)=108\\).</p>
