Kait rolls a fair 6-sided die until she rolls a 6. If she rolls a 6 on the \\(N\\)th roll, she then rolls the die \\(N\\) more times. What is the probability that she rolls a 6 during these next \\(N\\) times?

<details class="answer">

<summary>Solution</summary>

<div class="answer-body">

<p><strong>Answer:</strong> \\(\frac6\{11\}\\)</p>

<div class="solution-text">

<p>Consider the complementary event. If the first \\(6\\) occurs on turn \\(i\\), the previous \\(i-1\\) rolls and the next \\(i\\) rolls must all be different from \\(6\\). The probability of this is \\(\left(\frac56\right)^\{2i-1\}\cdot\frac16\\).</p>
<p>Sum over all \\(i\geq1\\). This is a geometric series with initial term \\(\frac5\{36\}\\) and common ratio \\(\frac\{25\}\{36\}\\):</p>
<p>\\[\Pr(\text\{no 6 in the next \}N\text\{ rolls\})=\frac\{5/36\}\{1-25/36\}=\frac5\{11\}.\\]</p>
<p>Hence the requested probability is \\(1-\frac5\{11\}=\frac6\{11\}\\).</p>

</div>
