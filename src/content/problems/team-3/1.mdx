In \\(\triangle ABC\\), the \\(A\\)-median, \\(B\\)-median, \\(C\\)-median have lengths \\(13,14,15\\), respectively. Find area of \\(\triangle ABC\\).

<details class="answer">

<summary>Solution</summary>

<div class="answer-body">

<p><strong>Answer:</strong> \\(112\\)</p>

<div class="solution-text">

<p>The triangle with side lengths \\(13,14,15\\) has semiperimeter \\(21\\) and area</p>
<p>\\[\sqrt\{21(21-13)(21-14)(21-15)\}=84.\\]</p>
<p>Let the area of \\(ABC\\) be \\(S\\). Reflect \\(B\\) across the midpoint of \\(AC\\) to obtain \\(D\\), so \\(ABCD\\) is a parallelogram. The centroid lies two-thirds of the way along each median. Comparing the resulting areas gives</p>
<p>\\[2S=6\left(\frac23\right)^2\cdot84,\qquad S=\frac43\cdot84=112.\\]</p>

</div>
