In triangle \\(\triangle ABC\\) with orthocenter \\(H\\), the internal angle bisector of \\(\angle BAC\\) intersects \\(\overline\{BC\}\\) at \\(Y\\). Given that \\(AH=4\\), \\(AY=6\\), and the distance from \\(Y\\) to \\(\overline\{AC\}\\) is \\(\sqrt\{15\}\\), compute \\(BC\\).

<details class="answer">

<summary>Solution</summary>

<div class="answer-body">

<p><strong>Answer:</strong> \\(4\sqrt\{35\}\\)</p>

<div class="solution-text">

<p>Let \\(E\\) be the foot of the altitude from \\(B\\) to \\(AC\\), and let \\(R\\) be the circumradius. Using the altitude relationships and the extended law of sines gives</p>
<p>\\[4=AH=\frac\{AE\}\{\sin\angle AHE\}=\frac\{AB\cos A\}\{\sin C\}=2R\cos A.\\]</p>
<p>The distance from \\(Y\\) to \\(AC\\) is \\(\sqrt\{15\}\\), while \\(AY=6\\). Thus \\(\cos\frac A2=\frac\{\sqrt\{21\}\}6\\), and</p>
<p>\\[\cos A=2\cos^2\frac A2-1=2\left(\frac\{\sqrt\{21\}\}6\right)^2-1=\frac16.\\]</p>
<p>Substituting gives \\(R=12\\) and \\(\sin A=\frac\{\sqrt\{35\}\}6\\). By the extended law of sines, \\(BC=2R\sin A=4\sqrt\{35\}\\).</p>
