Let \\(ABC\\) be an equilateral triangle. Let \\(P\\) be a point inside, such that \\(PA=6\\), \\(PB=12\\), and \\(PC=6\sqrt3\\). Find the side length of the triangle.

<details class="answer">

<summary>Solution</summary>

<div class="answer-body">

<p><strong>Answer:</strong> \\(6\sqrt7\\)</p>

<div class="solution-text">

<p>Rotate triangle \\(APC\\) by \\(60^\circ\\) counterclockwise to triangle \\(AP'B\\), taking \\(C\\) to \\(B\\). In triangle \\(APP'\\), \\(\angle P'AP=60^\circ\\) and \\(AP=AP'\\), so \\(PP'=6\\).</p>
<p>Triangle \\(PP'B\\) has side lengths \\(6,6\sqrt3,12\\), making it a \\(30\\)-\\(60\\)-\\(90\\) triangle. The angle relationships give \\(\angle APB=120^\circ\\). By the law of cosines,</p>
<p>\\[AB^2=6^2+12^2-2\cdot6\cdot12\cos120^\circ=252,\qquad AB=6\sqrt7.\\]</p>
