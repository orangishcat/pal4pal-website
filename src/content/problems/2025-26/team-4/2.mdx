Consider right \\(\triangle ABC\\) with \\(\angle C=90^\circ\\), \\(AB=10\\), and \\(AC=8\\). \\(CP=CQ=2\\). \\(BQ\\) and \\(AP\\) meet at \\(R\\). \\(CR\\) meet \\(AB\\) at \\(S\\). \\(QP\\) meet \\(AB\\) extension at \\(T\\). Find \\(TS\\).

<details class="answer">

<summary>Solution</summary>

<div class="answer-body">

<p><strong>Answer:</strong> \\(24\\)</p>

<div class="solution-text">

<p>The points \\(A,S,B,T\\) form a harmonic bundle, so \\(\frac\{AS\}\{BS\}=\frac\{AT\}\{BT\}\\). Alternatively, use Ceva's and Menelaus's theorems in \\(\triangle ABC\\).</p>
<p>Ceva's theorem gives \\(\frac\{AS\}\{BS\}=\frac32\\). Since \\(AB=10\\),</p>
<p>\\[\frac\{10+BT\}\{BT\}=\frac32,\qquad BT=20.\\]</p>
<p>Also \\(BS=4\\), so \\(TS=BT+BS=20+4=24\\).</p>

</div>
