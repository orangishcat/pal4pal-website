Let \\(n\\) be a positive integer. Find the sum of all possible values of \\(\gcd(5n+11,n+7)\\).

<details class="answer">

<summary>Solution</summary>

<div class="answer-body">

<p><strong>Answer:</strong> \\(60\\)</p>

<div class="solution-text">

<p>By the Euclidean algorithm,</p>
<p>\\[\gcd(5n+11,n+7)=\gcd(n+7,24).\\]</p>
<p>Every divisor of \\(24\\) can occur. They are \\(1,2,3,4,6,8,12,24\\), and their sum is \\(60\\).</p>
<p>Bonus problem from the PDF: solve the analogous problem for arbitrary \\(\gcd(an+b,cn+d)\\).</p>
