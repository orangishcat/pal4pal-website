For each integer \\(n\\), \\(1\<n\<500\\), there exists a number that is divisible by all positive integers less than or equal to \\(n\\) except \\(m\\) and \\(m+1\\). Find the sum of all possible \\(m\\).

<details class="answer">

<summary>Solution</summary>

<div class="answer-body">

<p><strong>Answer:</strong> \\(447\\)</p>

<div class="solution-text">

<p>The PDF argues that both omitted numbers \\(m\\) and \\(m+1\\) must be prime powers; otherwise, their proper factors would force them to divide the chosen number. Since they have different parity, one must be a power of \\(2\\).</p>
<p>The solution lists the candidate values \\(1,2,3,4,7,16,31,127,256\\). Adding them gives \\(447\\), the answer recorded in the PDF.</p>
