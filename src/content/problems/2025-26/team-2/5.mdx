Let \\(\triangle ABC\\) be a right triangle with right angle at \\(A\\) and \\(AC\\) the shorter leg. Point \\(D\\) lies inside the triangle such that \\(\angle ADC=90^\circ\\) and \\(\angle DCA=\angle ABC\\). Extend \\(BD\\) to a point \\(E\\) so that \\(\angle BEA=90^\circ\\) and \\(\angle DAE=\angle ABC\\). Find the ratio \\(BD:DE\\).

<details class="answer">

<summary>Solution</summary>

<div class="answer-body">

<p><strong>Answer:</strong> \\(3:1\\)</p>

<div class="solution-text">

<p>Use angle chasing, the angle bisector theorem, and the Pythagorean theorem. The solution PDF gives this hint and the answer \\(BD:DE=3:1\\).</p>
