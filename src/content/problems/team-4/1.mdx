Consider semicircle with diameter \\(AB\\), \\(C\\) is a point, \\(D\\) is the midpoint of arc \\(BC\\). \\(AD\\) meets \\(BC\\) at \\(E\\). If \\(CE=3\\), and \\(BD=2\sqrt5\\), find \\(AB\\).

<details class="answer">

<summary>Solution</summary>

<div class="answer-body">

<p><strong>Answer:</strong> \\(10\\)</p>

<div class="solution-text">

<p>Extend \\(AC\\) and \\(BD\\) to meet at \\(P\\). Since \\(D\\) is the midpoint of arc \\(BC\\), \\(DP=BD=2\sqrt5\\). The points \\(C,E,D,P\\) are concyclic.</p>
<p>Using the power of point \\(B\\),</p>
<p>\\[BE\cdot BC=BD\cdot BP=(2\sqrt5)(4\sqrt5)=40.\\]</p>
<p>Since \\(BC=BE+3\\), this gives \\(BE=5\\) and \\(BC=8\\). The construction gives \\(CP=4\\), and the solution concludes \\(AB=10\\).</p>

</div>
