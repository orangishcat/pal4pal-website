Kait rolls a fair 6-sided die until she rolls a 6. If she rolls a 6 on the $N$th roll, she then rolls the die $N$ more times. What is the probability that she rolls a 6 during these next $N$ times?

<details class="answer">

<summary>Solution</summary>

**Answer:** $\frac6{11}$

Consider the complementary event. If the first $6$ occurs on turn $i$, the previous $i-1$ rolls and the next $i$ rolls must all be different from $6$. The probability of this is $\left(\frac56\right)^{2i-1}\cdot\frac16$.

Sum over all $i\geq1$. This is a geometric series with initial term $\frac5{36}$ and common ratio $\frac{25}{36}$:

$$
\Pr(\text{no 6 in the next }N\text{ rolls})=\frac{5/36}{1-25/36}=\frac5{11}.
$$

Hence the requested probability is $1-\frac5{11}=\frac6{11}$.

</details>
