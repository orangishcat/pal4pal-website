Let $ABC$ be an equilateral triangle. Let $P$ be a point inside, such that $PA=6$, $PB=12$, and $PC=6\sqrt3$. Find the side length of the triangle.

<details class="answer">

<summary>Solution</summary>

**Answer:** $6\sqrt7$

Rotate triangle $APC$ by $60^\circ$ counterclockwise to triangle $AP'B$, taking $C$ to $B$. In triangle $APP'$, $\angle P'AP=60^\circ$ and $AP=AP'$, so $PP'=6$.

Triangle $PP'B$ has side lengths $6,6\sqrt3,12$, making it a $30$-$60$-$90$ triangle. The angle relationships give $\angle APB=120^\circ$. By the law of cosines,

$$
AB^2=6^2+12^2-2\cdot6\cdot12\cos120^\circ=252,\qquad AB=6\sqrt7.
$$

</details>
