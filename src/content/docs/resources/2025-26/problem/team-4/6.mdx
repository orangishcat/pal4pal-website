Let $n$ be a positive integer. Find the sum of all possible values of $\gcd(5n+11,n+7)$.

<details class="answer">

<summary>Solution</summary>

**Answer:** $60$

By the Euclidean algorithm,

$$
\gcd(5n+11,n+7)=\gcd(n+7,24).
$$

Every divisor of $24$ can occur. They are $1,2,3,4,6,8,12,24$, and their sum is $60$.
