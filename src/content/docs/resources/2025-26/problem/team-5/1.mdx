What is the remainder when $2^{202}+202$ is divided by $2^{101}+2^{51}+1$?

<details class="answer">

<summary>Solution</summary>

**Answer:** $201$

Complete the square, then apply the difference of squares:

$$
\begin{aligned}2^{202}+202&=(2^{101}+1)^2-2^{102}+201\\&=(2^{101}-2^{51}+1)(2^{101}+2^{51}+1)+201.\end{aligned}
$$

The product is divisible by the given divisor, leaving remainder $201$.
