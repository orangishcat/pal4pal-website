Consider semicircle with diameter $AB$, $C$ is a point, $D$ is the midpoint of arc $BC$. $AD$ meets $BC$ at $E$. If $CE=3$, and $BD=2\sqrt5$, find $AB$.

<details class="answer">

<summary>Solution</summary>

**Answer:** $10$

Extend $AC$ and $BD$ to meet at $P$. Since $D$ is the midpoint of arc $BC$, $DP=BD=2\sqrt5$. The points $C,E,D,P$ are concyclic.

Using the power of point $B$,

$$
BE\cdot BC=BD\cdot BP=(2\sqrt5)(4\sqrt5)=40.
$$

Since $BC=BE+3$, this gives $BE=5$ and $BC=8$. The construction gives $CP=4$, and the solution concludes $AB=10$.

</details>
