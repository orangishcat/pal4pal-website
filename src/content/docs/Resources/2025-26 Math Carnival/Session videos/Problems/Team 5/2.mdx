Isosceles trapezoid $ABCD$ has parallel sides $AD$ and $BC$, with $BC\lt AD$ and $AB=CD$. There is a point $P$ in the plane such that $PA=1$, $PB=2$, $PC=3$, and $PD=4$. What is $\frac{BC}{AD}$?

<details class="answer">

<summary>Solution</summary>

**Answer:** $\frac13$

Reflect $P$ over the perpendicular bisector of $BC$ to obtain $P'$. Then $P'A=PD=4$, $P'D=PA=1$, $P'C=PB=2$, and $P'B=PC=3$. The isosceles trapezoids $DAPP'$ and $CBPP'$ are cyclic.

Apply Ptolemy's theorem to each:

$$
PP'\cdot AD+1\cdot1=4\cdot4,\qquad PP'\cdot BC+2\cdot2=3\cdot3.
$$

Thus $PP'\cdot AD=15$ and $PP'\cdot BC=5$. Dividing gives $\frac{BC}{AD}=\frac5{15}=\frac13$.

</details>
